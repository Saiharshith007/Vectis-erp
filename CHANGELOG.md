## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-04

First public release.

### Added

- Purchase orders, quotations, proforma and tax invoices, with PDFs on your own
  letterhead, logo, signature and stamp
- Received purchase orders, billed in parts through proforma invoices
- Receipts (including part payments and advances), credit notes and sales returns
- GST as CGST + SGST or IGST, chosen from the company and client GSTIN state codes
- Export invoices in foreign currency, with the exchange rate stored per invoice
- Dashboard, sales register and outstanding summary, with PDF and Excel export
- Bulk import of clients, suppliers and past invoices from CSV or Excel,
  including Tally Prime ledger exports
- Staff sign-up with admin approval and an append-only activity log
- JSON-file storage out of the box; PostgreSQL and Supabase Storage for hosted
  setups
